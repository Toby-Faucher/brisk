# Sticker

Bright flat colours, 3px black outlines, hard offset shadows, slightly tilted cards. The sidebar becomes a top bar of fat
pill buttons, one colour each. `Layer.svelte` draws the confetti shapes in the page edges (not on /stream).

Fonts (self-hosted, exact pins, imported in theme.css):
- `@fontsource-variable/fredoka` 5.3.0 (headings, buttons, nav)
- `@fontsource-variable/nunito` 5.3.0 (body)

Accent: the Settings swatches still work. Each accent maps to a darker shade here so white text stays readable.

Needs shared change: none.
