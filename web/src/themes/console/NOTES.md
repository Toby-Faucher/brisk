# Console shelf

Deep navy to violet, round tiles across the top, big covers on a shelf, a hint bar at the bottom.

- Fonts: `@fontsource-variable/fredoka@5.3.0` (headings, labels) and `@fontsource-variable/nunito@5.3.0` (text). Both self-hosted.
- `Layer.svelte` draws the hint bar (Enter, Esc, /, Ctrl K, Alt U). All of those keys exist today. Hidden on /stream.
- The "Coming" entries in the sidebar are hidden (they are not controls).
